# Evidence check, 20 November 2026: addendum (passages read in full source texts after the four evidence files were written)

The second referee could not find six figures in the evidence files. Each was read in the full text of its source; the passages are quoted here so that the record is complete.

## IRENA (2025), Renewable Power Generation Costs in 2024 (full report)

- Solar cost shares: "In the markets assessed in Figure 3.4, 2024 saw – on average – the BoS share (excluding inverters) make up around 65% of TICs." "Overall, soft cost categories for the countries evaluated made up 30% of total BoS costs and, on average, 20% of TICs during 2024. Modules and inverters together (non-BoS costs) ranged from USD 197/kW to USD 504/kW, with their share ranging from 16% to 49%. The average share for that category was 35%." "BoS hardware components made up between 22% and 50% of TICs during 2024, with an average share of 33% (equivalent to USD 239/kW). Installation costs ranged between 10% and 28% of TICs, giving an average of 18%, or USD 151/kW." (TIC = total installed cost.)
- Wind: "At an aggregated level, wind turbines are the largest cost driver. On average, they account for approximately 75% of total installed costs (IEA Wind, 2011)." Note the underlying source is from 2011.
- Batteries: "China dominates supply, producing over 75% of global batteries at costs 20–30% lower than in European and North American markets (IEA, 2025a)" (summary for policy makers).

## IEA (2021), Net Zero by 2050

- "Around 8% of emissions reductions stem from behavioural changes and materials efficiency".

## IBRD (2024), Portfolio Historical Borrower Default Experience

- IBRD has experienced no "loss of contractual interest or principal in all its past" cases and "has two outstanding non-accrual cases".

## What stays unverified at first hand

- The Egypt values of the cost of capital (9.71 percent nominal after tax; 8.8 percent real benchmark) were read from a published database that transcribes the IRENA report's data, not from the report itself. The currency of the nominal rate is not recorded; the note treats it as a dollar rate and says so.
- Reversals of carbon prices outside the European Union (Australia 2014, France 2018, Canada 2025) were seen only in search summaries and are not cited.
