# Where "A Solar Economy Financing System" went

"A Solar Economy Financing System" (Stephen Stretton, Jasper Zimmermann) made two
proposals that have been separated, because they address different risks and
belong in different papers.

**Part (i), the multi-tiered guarantee structure for green bonds**, is now
Section 5 ("Where there is no contract: tiered bond guarantees") of
**3.2 From Projects to Asset Classes: Two-Stage Finance and Synthetic Sovereign
Bonds for Clean Power**, as the case for system assets that have no power
purchase agreement to guarantee: transmission, storage, equipment factories.
The conditionality and the local-currency point went with it. The two-stage
finance structure itself is the core of the same paper.

**Part (ii), regional clean energy equity funds**, is now the regional funds of
**4.2 The Climate Investment Trust: Hold Capital, Lend Its Strength** (its
Section 6), which hold the first-loss tranche of the 3.2 portfolios. The
earlier plan for a separate paper "2.4 Equity De-risking for Clean Energy" was
dropped in the 29 October 2026 series restructuring.

The reason for splitting rather than merging: 3.1 (Guarantee the Contract, Not
the Debt) argues that guaranteeing the offtake contract is better than
guaranteeing the debt, because it is systemic rather than transactional and
preserves recourse. This paper guaranteed the debt. The two are not in conflict
once the domain is stated (guarantee the contract where there is one, guarantee
the bond where there is not), but they are different mechanisms and were being
described as one.
