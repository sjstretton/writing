# Source digests for series 2 and 3 (29 Oct 2026)

Agent-written digests of all source material for the series 2 (Finance and Risk) and series 3 (Sovereign Incentives) upgrade, plus the common instructions given to the writing agents. Plans: `2.FinanceAndRisk/SeriesArchitecture.md`, `3.SovereignIncentives/SeriesArchitecture.md`.

- `digest-papers.md`: the papers, briefs, archives and a repo-wide search. Summaries of World Bank internal documents have been removed.
- `digest-books.md`: *Time and Chance* and *The Right Policy at the Wrong Time*, chapter by chapter, with §11 mapping chapters to papers.
- `WRITER-BRIEF.md`: the instructions for the per-paper writing agents (brief + working paper). The pandoc CSL path in it is a session scratch file; download `https://www.zotero.org/styles/chicago-author-date` if it is missing.
