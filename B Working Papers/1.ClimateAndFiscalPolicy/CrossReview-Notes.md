# Cross-review notes for the carbon-pricing series

Issues found while drafting, to resolve in the cross-review pass across all briefs and working papers.

1. **Electricity across the two tracks (from the 1.2 brief agent).** 1.1 puts generation on the production track (intensity metric, feebate; detail in 1.2) but also lists "power sold to the grid" under the consumption track's forward obligation. Resolution: generation is priced by the feebate (production track); electricity sold to final users bears the consumption obligation on its embodied carbon, net of carbon prices paid upstream (1.6 brief already says this). Fix the wording in the 1.1 brief and working paper.
2. **Series titles.** Update the CFPB entries in every brief and working paper .bib to the final titles: 1.2 "Feebates: The Full Carbon Price at the Margin, Without the Carbon Bill"; 1.6 "Carbon Consumption Tax: A Carbon Component in VAT and a Forward Obligation on Energy Suppliers"; 1.3 and 1.7 when final.
3. **1.6 brief refers to "the working paper" (Stephen's earlier v0.1).** Rephrase so the brief stands alone.
4. **Feebate abatement relative to a tax.** IMF Fiscal Monitor (Oct 2019) puts feebate packages at roughly 50–70 percent of a tax's emission cuts (per the 1.2 brief agent; verify). Cite in 1.1 Section 5 alongside Proposition 4.
5. **French bonus-malus.** It cost €285m in 2008 and, per D'Haultfœuille et al., raised net emissions: a lesson on benchmark updating. Make sure no paper calls it costless.
6. **1.1 brief Proposition numbering** (1–5) differs from the 1.1 working paper (1–7). Fine, but cross-references between papers must name the result, not the number.
