# Carbon-pricing series: what each document is called

An index of every document related to 1.1, with its real title as written inside it. Several file names and YAML titles differ from the real title. All files listed were opened and read in full text on 29 Oct 2026 (Word through pandoc, PDF through pdftotext, PowerPoint through python-pptx; spreadsheets by sheet names only). Word counts are approximate. Paths are relative to `B Working Papers/`.
