## University of Oxford: DPhil in Geography and the Environment

School of Geography and the Environment; supervised by Dr Shrimali and the Oxford Sustainable Finance Group. Course page: <https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/dphil-geography-and-environment>. [General application guide](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/application-guide?source=coursepage).

Five inputs:

1. **Research proposal**, about 2,500 words ([guidance](https://www.geog.ox.ac.uk/study/graduate/apply/research_proposal.html)). The count excludes bibliography and brief footnotes; show it on the document. Sections: title, background and rationale, research questions, theoretical framework/methodology, methods. Show capability and the work plan: a schedule with the intended finish date and progress points. Name the supervisor and state their agreement and suitability. Ethics only if needed (CUREC checklist; likely not).
2. **Written work**, one piece, 2,000 words maximum excluding bibliography and footnotes, word count shown. An essay, thesis chapter, published paper or something written for the application. Need not relate to the proposal.
3. **Official transcripts** from prior degrees. Josh: "are they in a box in the UK??"
4. **Three referees**, at least two academic; names go on the application.
5. **Online form**, which collects CV details. Do not upload a CV.

Other points:
- Contact the supervisor before applying, with CV and draft proposal; a preliminary agreement is needed before you name them on the form.
- The site says only listed individuals can be primary supervisor. Ask Dr Shrimali whether he can be, or whom he recommends: [Dr Ben Caldecott](https://www.smithschool.ox.ac.uk/person/dr-ben-caldecott), [Prof Doyne Farmer](https://www.inet.ox.ac.uk/people/j-doyne-farmer), [Prof Cameron Hepburn](https://www.smithschool.ox.ac.uk/person/professor-cameron-hepburn).
- Applications for 2027-28 were expected to open Wed 16 Sep 2026. Create a graduate application account to reach the form.
- Fees 2027-28: £25,890 a year full-time, £12,945 part-time. Application fee £20.
- DPhil by publication: no clear answer for this course; [other departments](https://www.mpls.ox.ac.uk/graduate-school/information-for-postgraduate-research-students/submitting-your-thesis/guidance-for-submitting-an-integrated-thesis) offer an integrated thesis. Ask Dr Shrimali or <research-degrees-coordinator@ouce.ox.ac.uk>.
- Josh's assessment (before cleanup): the proposal was near-ready, needing minor grammar fixes. Finished materials go in one OneDrive applications folder.
