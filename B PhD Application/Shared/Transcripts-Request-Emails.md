# Transcript request emails

Prepared 1 Nov 2026. Used by every application (UCL, Oxford, Cambridge), so it sits in `Shared/`. Fill every `[...]` before sending. I have not looked up any office, address or fee, so each route is marked `[VERIFY]`.

## What is needed and why

- **UCL** (per a search extract of another UCL department's guide, unverified): module grades plus the overall grade; a certificate alone is not enough. Documents cannot be replaced or added after you submit.
- **Oxford** (per search extracts, unverified): grades from all university-level qualifications.
- **Order of work:** ask for the copies now; the CV still has `[FILL IN]` for the MPhil result, the MA result and the BA class, and the transcripts will settle them.

## 0. First check the box

Josh's note asks whether the transcripts are "in a box in the UK". If someone holds the box, one message may save the wait.

> Hi [Name], could you look in the box of my university papers for any transcripts or degree certificates (Cambridge BA, Part III, MPhil; Birkbeck MA) and send me phone photos of every page? I need them for PhD applications this month. Thank you.

Even photos of old transcripts may be enough to start the online forms (unverified; the offer stage will need official copies).
