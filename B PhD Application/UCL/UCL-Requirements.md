## Proposal guidance

Two-page outline (Step 1, for potential supervisors) covers:

- background
- key literature
- research question(s)
- proposed research methods

Full proposal (Step 4, for the formal application):

- "This would normally be at least 6 pages in length, expanding on your outline proposal and including your supervisory team, potential publications and a timeline for the PhD research."
- Supervisors "will normally give you feedback about how you can improve your full proposal prior to the formal application stage."

Exemplar full proposal ("Joe Bloggs"; 6 pages, about 2,200 words including references):

1. Proposal Title
2. Background and research questions. This includes a numbered list of four aims, and states what is novel.
3. Methodology. It links the work to the applicant's Master's research.
4. Supervisory team. Names the principal supervisor (ISR) and a secondary supervisor (another UCL institute) with one line each on their fit. It also lists an external honorary adviser.
5. Potential publications. Four candidate papers.
6. Proposed Timetable. Key dates (start, upgrade report, second-year report, paper submissions, thesis submission) and a three-year Gantt chart.
7. References

Notable points in the exemplar:

- It ties the PhD to an existing funded project and named external partners.
- It sets dated milestones, including the upgrade report. A part-time applicant should adapt this to five years, with the upgrade at about 15 months.
