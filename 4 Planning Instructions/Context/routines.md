# Routines (regular lists)

Same as the Regular lists tab of the master Sheet, and printed at the end of `Me-Printable-Tracker.docx`.
Format: `## List` starts a printed page, `### List` is a list on that page; `- [ ] label | item` (label in bold, optional).
List names match the Sheet's headings (ignoring case). A `##` heading with no items (Bags) is a page title only.

## Morning (an hour from waking; then full Kriya and breakfast 8-9)
- [ ] 1 | Wake: wash; light on
- [ ] 2 | Stretch: yoga, Kriya, meditation
- [ ] 3 | Drink
- [ ] 4 | Do: name the one focused thing, then start it
- [ ] 8-9 | Full Kriya, then breakfast

### Gym, longer session (~30 min)
- [ ] 1 | 2k on the ergo: note the time
- [ ] 2 | 3 rounds: press-ups, squats, cross crawl (30 reps each)
- [ ] 3 | Log total time in the Tracker

## Evening
- [ ] 1 | Hot shower
- [ ] 2 | Pulsetto; food if needed
- [ ] 3 | Tidy (10 min)
- [ ] 4 | Wash up; laundry if needed
- [ ] 5 | Pack and check the main bag (below)
- [ ] 6 | Sleep protocol (below)
- [ ] 7 | Dashboard: write tomorrow's 1-3 questions, then answer them (Decide tonight)
- [ ] 8 | End of day: tick what is done (anywhere: Plan, Dashboard, lists or Tracker), then ask Claude for the end-of-day tidy

### Anxiety + sleep protocol
- [ ] Body | Child, Butterfly, Legs up wall, Roll body
- [ ] Breath | Alternate nostril, Straw breath, Bhramari
- [ ] Then | Panchakosha or Yoga Nidra

## Bags

### Main bag (every morning)
- [ ] Work | Laptop, laptop charger
- [ ] Power | Battery pack, charging wire
- [ ] Phones | Two phones
- [ ] Me | Wallet, keys, WB pass
- [ ] Swim | Trunks, goggles
- [ ] Spare | Boxers, socks, trousers, shirt
- [ ] Audio | JLab earbuds x2
- [ ] Plan | Kanban sheet, with notes (if available)
- [ ] Food | Breakfast: nuts, orange, banana
- [ ] Shop | One shopping bag
- [ ] Warm | Hats, gloves etc.

### Grab bag (rare backup)
- [ ] Swim | Trunks, goggles
- [ ] Device | iPod touch (second recording device)
