# Reference check for 6.1, working paper v0.4 and brief v1.1 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself: the abstract at minimum (Crossref, OpenAlex, IDEAS/RePEc, NBER, IMF and publisher pages), and the passage where a specific number is cited (the IMF working paper 22/254 was downloaded and searched; the 2019 Fiscal Monitor text was read). Where paper 5.4's reference check (same day) had already read a shared source, its reading was reused and is marked "via paper 5.4" in the entry's note. Each entry's `note` in `../PolicyEquivalentCarbonPriceAndCES.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the keys the brief cites. No personal data was sent in any request.

Totals: 53 entries in the working paper's `.bib` (44 external sources; 9 series and working-paper entries). 42 external sources carried over from v0.3 and re-checked; 2 added (Weil 2021; Labandeira, Labeaga and López 2016). 14 passages re-worded because the source said something different or less; 1 source cut from the text (Abrell, Kosch and Rausch 2019, never read); 10 further unused v0.3 entries, most with old `VERIFY` notes, removed from the `.bib` (they remain in `8.PreviousVersions/PolicyEquivalentCarbonPriceAndCES-v0.3.bib`).
