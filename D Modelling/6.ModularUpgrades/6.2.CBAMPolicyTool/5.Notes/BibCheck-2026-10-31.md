# Reference check for 6.2, working paper v0.2 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself on 31 Oct 2026. The EU legal texts were retrieved in full from the EU Publications Office (Regulations 2023/956 and 2025/2083; Implementing Regulations 2025/2547, 2025/2620, 2025/2621 and 2026/1740; Directive 2023/959) and the Commission's Have Your Say portal (the draft implementing regulation Ares(2026)4841230 and its annex, with the portal API for its status). The academic sources were re-checked against Crossref by DOI; their abstracts and, where noted, full texts were read in paper 1.4's check of the same date, and the entries are kept identical to 1.4's so the two papers cannot drift apart. Eurostat Comext was re-queried. The IPCC chapters were read in the PDF. Each entry's `note` in `../CBAMPolicyTool.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the keys it cites.

The working paper cites 35 keys, including five series entries; all were checked. Four sources led to a correction of the text, five were added, and fifteen uncited or duplicate entries were removed. The brief cites 16 keys, all from the working paper's file.
