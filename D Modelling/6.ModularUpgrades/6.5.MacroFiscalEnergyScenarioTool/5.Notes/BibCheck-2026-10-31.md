# Reference check for 6.5, working paper v0.2 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself on 31 Oct 2026: bibliographic details on Crossref (by DOI) and the publisher's page; content from the abstract at least, and from the passage where the claim is specific (a number, a threshold, a rating). Public data were read from the providers' own APIs or files. Each entry's `note` in `../MacroFiscalEnergyScenarioTool.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the keys the brief cites. No personal data was sent in any request.

Not reachable from this session: the private Climate Fiscal AI Tools repository (so the tool's own Kenya numbers were checked against the saved extraction, `2.Source/kenya_run_output.txt`, and for internal consistency); pefa.org (403), so Kenya's PEFA score could not be read; the Kenya Treasury website (certificate error), so its October 2024 DSA copy was not read and the IMF's December 2023 DSA is cited instead.
