# Reference check for 7.1, working paper v0.4 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself: the abstract at minimum, and the passage or table where a specific number is cited. Bibliographic fields were checked against Crossref, the DOI resolver or the publisher's record. Each entry's `note` in `../ExciseFiscalDiagnostic.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the keys the brief cites. No query carried an email address or other personal data. The country results come only from the diagnostic's own workbook extract (`2.Source/country_metrics_SLE_LBR.csv`), as cleared in the README; no text from the "draft for discussion" or "not for citation" country documents is used.

Totals: 33 entries in the working paper's `.bib`: 26 external sources, 6 series entries (including the new CFPB71 key for this brief and CFPB54) and the diagnostic's workbook. Of the 12 external sources in v0.3, all 12 were re-checked and kept (4 with corrected fields); 14 added; 7 unused sibling entries removed (StrettonWP52, 53, 62, 63, 64, 65, 71). About a dozen sentences re-worded because the source said something different or less; 5 unsourced reference values cut or replaced.
