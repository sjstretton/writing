# Reference check for 7.2, working paper v0.2 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself on 31 Oct 2026: bibliographic details on Crossref (DOIs) and OpenAlex; content from the abstract at least, and from the passage or the data where the claim is specific. Datasets were checked by reading the data through the publishers' public interfaces (IMF DataMapper API, IMF SDMX API for WoRLD, Ember's long-format CSV) or, where a page blocks automated reading, through the publisher's own descriptions found by search. Each entry's `note` in `../FiscalIndicatorsDashboard.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the keys the brief cites, plus the series entry CFPB52. No personal data (no email address) was sent in any request.

The build pipeline and panel sit in the private Climate Fiscal AI Tools repository, which was not available; Table 2 was rechecked against the publishers instead (`../2.Source/check_public_sources.py` and its output).
