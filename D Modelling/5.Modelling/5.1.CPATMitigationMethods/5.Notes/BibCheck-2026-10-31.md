# Reference check for 5.1, working paper v0.2 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself on 31 Oct 2026: Crossref for the bibliographic record (no personal data sent), then the abstract or the passage that the text relies on, from the publisher, IDEAS/RePEc, the IMF and World Bank sites, PMLR and arXiv. The CPAT documentation and the CPAT-AI registers and equation list were read in a fresh clone of the public repository `cpmodelai/cpat_ai` (commit 108f976). Each entry's `note` in `../CPATMitigationMethods.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the keys the brief cites.
