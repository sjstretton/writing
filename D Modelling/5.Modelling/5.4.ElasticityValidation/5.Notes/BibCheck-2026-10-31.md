# Reference check for 5.4, working paper v0.2 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself: the abstract at minimum, and the table or passage where a specific number is cited. Bibliographic fields were checked against Crossref, OpenAlex or the publisher's record. Each entry's `note` in `../ElasticityValidation.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's, for exactly the keys the brief cites. CPAT material comes only from public sources: the public CPAT-AI repository (cloned at commit 108f976; its documentation, public data and validation files) and the World Bank's publicly disclosed 2022 CPAT technical notes. No confidential, internal or not-for-citation material is used.

Totals: 32 entries in the working paper's `.bib` (28 external sources; 4 series entries, including the new CFPB54 key for this brief). 22 external sources carried over from v0.1, all re-checked; 6 added; 2 unused sibling entries removed. 11 sentences re-worded because the source said something different or less; none cut entirely (Galindo et al. kept only as second-hand, labeled so).
