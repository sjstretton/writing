# PAPERS.md

The writing program: which papers to upgrade, in what order, and which new papers to write. There are four lists.

1. **Priority papers**: existing papers to bring to the paper standard.
2. **Introductory papers**: short papers that introduce each area and its main results.
3. **Concept papers**: one concept each, written to enter World Bank discourse.
4. **Position papers**: the overarching papers that justify Stephen's positions.

Status key: `idea` · `notes` · `draft` · `upgrading` · `standard` (passes `E Writing Standards/paper-standard.md`) · `submitted` · `published`.

## 3. Concept papers

A concept paper introduces one idea into World Bank discourse. It is 1,500–2,500 words, names the concept, defines it in one sentence, shows one worked example and says what changes in operations if people adopt it. The aim is that colleagues start using the term. Write them in this order, so later concepts can build on earlier ones.

| Order | Concept | One-line definition (draft) | Home paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Policy-equivalent carbon price | The carbon price that would cut emissions as much as a given policy package does. | 4.1 |
| 2 | The two-plus-one functions of carbon pricing | Carbon pricing does three jobs (production incentive, consumption signal and revenue) that different instruments can do better separately. | 1.1 |
| 3 | Destination-based carbon pricing | Charge carbon where goods are consumed, as VAT does, so exports are zero-rated and imports pay. | 1.1 |
| 4 | Feebate as benchmark plus price | A feebate is a sectoral emissions-intensity benchmark plus a price on deviation from it. | 1.2 |
| 5 | Offtaker risk | The risk that the buyer of power does not pay; the main driver of clean power's cost of capital. | 2.1 |
| 6 | Payment intermediary | A facility that pays generators on time whether or not the distributor pays, backed by a guarantee waterfall. | 2.1 |
| 7 | Synthetic sovereign bonds | Bonds rated at developed-sovereign level because developed countries co-guarantee them. | 2.1 |
| 8 | Equity as collateral | One pool of equity that both invests in projects and stands as first-loss collateral for others. | 2.2 |
| 9 | Notional capitalised value | A climate balance credited to a country for its carbon stocks, usable as guarantee support and used up by business-as-usual emissions. | 3.1 |
| 10 | The externality spine | A common scale that puts carbon, air pollution, accidents, congestion and road damage side by side. | 5.1 |
