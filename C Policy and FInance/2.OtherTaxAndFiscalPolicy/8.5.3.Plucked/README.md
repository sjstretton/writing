# 8.5.3 Plucked! A guide to a fair, simple, efficient and politically feasible tax and benefits system

Book manuscript by Stephen Stretton and Gordon Styles (2020), about 102,000 words in 14 chapters: a case for reforming UK tax and benefits (tax principles and practice, welfare and work, inequality, corporations, land and property, money and debt, environment and resources). Imported on 31 Oct 2026 from the private repository `sjstretton/screwed` (last edited Feb 2020).

Title: retitled on 31 Oct 2026. The 2020 title was *Screwed?! Getting our future back with tax and benefit reform*, and earlier working titles were *Revolutionising Taxation and Welfare*, *Tax Heaven* and *The Operating System of Capitalism*. The new title borrows the image of taxation as plucking the goose, which chapter 2 quotes. Only the title has changed; the 2020 text is otherwise as written.
