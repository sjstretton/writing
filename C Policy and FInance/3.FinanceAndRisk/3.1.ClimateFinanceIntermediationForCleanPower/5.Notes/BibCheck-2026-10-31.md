## Checks of claims in other papers of the series (requested by the coordinator)

The note in `H Writing Instructions/TODO.md` (29 Oct) that "series 3 now leads with the MDB loss record (~80 per donor dollar; 2.7 stress case); TFFF cited to its Concept Note 3.0" refers to the old numbering: these claims are in papers 4.1 and 4.2 (old 3.1 and 3.2), not in this paper. Checked against the sources, read-only:

- *About 80 dollars per donor dollar.* 4.2 uses an annual MDB sovereign default rate of 0.77 percent and recovery of 95.1 percent (GEMs, 2025). Both figures are confirmed by the EIB's GEMs release of October 2025 and by secondary reports; the EIB notes the 0.77 percent partly reflects a change in GEMs reporting methodology, and its publication page labels the period 1994–2024 while the release says 41 years (1984–2024). Arithmetic: $0.03 / (0.0077 \times 0.049) = 79.5$. Confirmed.
- *2.7 in the stress case.* Calls at 3 percent a year and a loss of 37 percent, the average sovereign haircut in Cruces and Trebesch (2013), confirmed (180 cases, 1970–2010; a 2024 NBER update over 200 years finds about 45 percent). $0.03 / (0.03 \times 0.37) = 2.70$. Confirmed.
- *TFFF Concept Note 3.0.* Read (Spanish edition): about \$4 per hectare a year for countries with deforestation at or below 0.5 percent; deduction of 100 hectares per hectare deforested below 0.3 percent and 200 between 0.3 and 0.5 percent; 1:35 for fire degradation; at least 20 percent to Indigenous peoples and local communities; \$25 billion of sponsor (junior) capital and up to \$100 billion of senior debt; sponsor cost aligned with long-term US Treasury yields; World Bank as expected trustee. All confirmed. **One discrepancy:** 4.2 (and 4.1, citing Barber 2025) describe sponsor capital as "40-year loans". Concept Note 3.0 says the repayment term was *reduced to 30 years*, with a ten-year grace period. If 4.1 and 4.2 cite the Concept Note for the term, it should be 30 years; the 40 years may come from earlier TFFF material.
