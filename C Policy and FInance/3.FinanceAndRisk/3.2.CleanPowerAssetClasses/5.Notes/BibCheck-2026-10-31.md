# Reference check for 3.2, working paper v0.2 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every outside source cited in either form (38 in all, 29 carried over and 9 added) was checked against the source itself: the full text where it was reachable (Moody's 2017, the Vietnam grid brief), otherwise the abstract or the publisher's page, with bibliographic details checked against Crossref, RePEc, OSTI, BIS, IFC, FMO, PIDG, RBI and the law firms' transaction notes. Each entry's `note` field in `../CleanPowerAssetClasses.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the 15 keys the brief cites. The series' own papers (CFPB and StrettonWP keys) and the author's manuscript *Time and Chance* are not outside sources; claims resting only on *Time and Chance* are now labeled as the author's estimates, and public facts it was cited for (Vietnam, South Africa) now cite public sources.

The series 4 headline (about 80 dollars of incentive per donor dollar on the MDB sovereign loss record; 2.7 in the stress case) is not quoted in 3.2, so there was no such claim to check here. The working paper now says explicitly that its mezzanine co-guarantee cost is a different ratio.
