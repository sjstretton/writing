# Reference check for 4.3, working paper v0.2 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every outside source cited in either form (35 in all: 21 carried over and 14 added) was checked against the source itself: the full text where it could be reached (the Uruguay ministry's briefing note, the SEC prospectus supplement, Feldhütter et al. 2024, the World Bank Group Evolution Roadmap, Lefournier 2023, the Cruces–Trebesch working paper), otherwise the abstract, the publisher's or issuer's page, or the issuer's own release, with bibliographic details checked against Crossref and RePEc. Each entry's `note` field in `../SustainabilityLinkedLending.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the 22 keys the brief cites. The series' own papers (CFPB and StrettonWP keys) are not outside sources. No confidential, internal or draft-for-discussion material was used; the World Bank internal paper in `9.Archive/` was not opened. No personal data was sent in any request.
