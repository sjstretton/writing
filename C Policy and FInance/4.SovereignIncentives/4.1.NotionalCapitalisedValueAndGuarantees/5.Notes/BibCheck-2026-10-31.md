# Reference check for 4.1, working paper v0.2 and brief v1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself: the abstract at minimum, and the passage where the text quotes a number, a date or a rule. Bibliographic details were checked against Crossref (by DOI), publisher and repository pages, and the official PDFs (TFFF Concept Note 3.0, Guyana's LCDS 2030, the ART validation report for Guyana, the ICVCM HFLD observations, IPCC 2006). Each entry's `note` in `../NotionalCapitalisedValueAndGuarantees.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the keys the brief cites. No personal data was sent in any request.

Totals: 35 external sources checked (25 cited in v0.1, 10 new). Passages relying on 9 sources re-worded; 3 sources cut or replaced (OilNOW, the Reuters reprint, the IBRD filing); 10 added. The two forms now cite 32 external sources. The author's own manuscripts (*Time and Chance*, *The Right Policy at the Wrong Time*, the DPhil proposal) and the series keys (CFPB31–61, StrettonWP42–43) were checked for title consistency only.
