# Reference check for 1.3, working paper v0.3 and brief v1.1 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked. Sources shared with paper 1.1 and verified there on 31 Oct 2026 were copied from `../../1.1.OpenEconomyCarbonPricing/OpenEconomyCarbonPricing.bib` (with their notes) and the claims made of them here were compared with 1.1's verified wording. Every other source was checked against the source itself on 31 Oct 2026 (legal texts against the Official Journal through the Publications Office, CELEX numbers in the notes) or carries the record of the 29 Oct 2026 check, which read at least the abstract. Each entry's `note` in `../CarbonConsumptionTax.bib` says what was read and when. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's for exactly the keys it cites.

Totals: 82 sources cited in the working paper, 78 of them outside the series (the brief cites 20, all among them). 10 added, 13 cut, 22 re-worded or re-dated. No `[VERIFY]` marker remains in either master or either `.bib`.
