# Reference check for 1.1, working paper v0.4 and brief v1.1 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form whose content had not been read before was checked against the source itself (abstract at minimum; the relevant passage where the claim is specific), and bibliographic details were checked against Crossref, publisher pages, EUR-Lex, the World Bank and IMF repositories. Each entry's `note` field in `../OpenEconomyCarbonPricing.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is now copied from the working paper's, so the two cannot drift apart.

## Calibration

The base price of \$600/t is now sourced as lying between the 2025 world export price of hot-rolled band (about \$440–485/t) and the Western European price (about \$630–750/t). The Armington elasticity is now the estimate for ferrous metals, 5.9, in place of the unsourced 4; the race-to-the-top results are reported at both (the conclusion does not change).
