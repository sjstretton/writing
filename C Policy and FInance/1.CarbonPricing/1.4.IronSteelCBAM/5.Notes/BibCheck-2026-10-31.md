# Reference check for 1.4, working paper v0.4 and brief v1.1 (31 Oct 2026)

Every source cited in either form was checked against the source itself on 31 Oct 2026: the legal texts in full from the EU Publications Office and the Commission's Have Your Say portal; academic sources against Crossref metadata and at least the abstract (OpenAlex, publisher pages), and the passage itself where the text makes a specific claim (Sandbag 2024, IEA 2020 and Mehling and Ritz 2023 were read in full). Each entry's `note` in `../IronSteelCBAM.bib` records what was read. The brief's `.bib` is copied from the working paper's, so the two cannot drift apart. Sources that 1.1 verified the same day (Directive 2023/959, COM(2026) 616, Neuhoff et al. 2026, Mattoo et al. 2009 and the papers read in full in that session) were re-checked against Crossref and their abstracts here.

The working paper cites 53 keys (plus the series entries); all 53 were checked. Of these, 12 led to a change in the text, 7 were added, and 5 entries were cut. The brief cites 18 keys, all from the working paper's verified file.
