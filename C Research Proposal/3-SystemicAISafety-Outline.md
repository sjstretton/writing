Status: outline, 29 Oct 2026. This is a scaffold for the second PhD application, which `A Writing Instructions/STRATEGY.md` makes the next writing priority. It sets out the structure and the decisions Stephen needs to make. It contains no claims yet; everything substantive is a question.

## Decisions for Stephen first

The draft cannot start until these are answered.

1. **Core question.** In one sentence, what does the PhD ask? For example (agent suggestions only): how incentive and institutional design can reduce risks that arise from the interaction of many AI systems and organizations, rather than from any single model.
2. **Angle.** Which of Stephen's strengths does it lean on? Options: mechanism and incentive design (from the sovereign-incentives work); public finance and regulation (taxes, liability, insurance, guarantees for AI risk); modelling of systemic risk (from climate and financial-stability analogies); philosophy of agency and institutions.
3. **Programs and supervisors.** Which universities, departments and supervisors? Each must be checked on live pages.
4. **Relation to proposal 1.** Parallel application, or the fallback? This decides how much effort goes in before January.
5. **Deadlines.** Per program, verified.

## Planned structure (~2,500 words)

Follows the "Research proposal" type in `E Writing Standards/writing-types.md`.

1. **Title.** States the question.
2. **The problem.** What "systemic" AI risk is, in plain words, with a concrete scenario. Why model-level safety work does not address it.
3. **Research questions.** Two or three, each answerable in three years.
4. **Literature and gap.** Systemic risk in finance and climate (the analogy Stephen knows well); AI governance and safety literature; mechanism design. Verified sources only.
5. **Approach and methods.** What is modelled, what is estimated and what data exist.
6. **Contribution.** What the field will know at the end that it does not know now.
7. **Feasibility and timeline.** Three years; what Stephen brings (economics, modelling, policy institutions).
8. **Fit.** Why this department and this supervisor.
