Peers: What are peers in terms of population density, emissions, and development level?
Balance: What is the balance of green vs brown expenditure/investment and resilient/non-resilient spending? 
Allocative Efficiency: How does my spending match up to the sectoral needs (big chunks: emissions and climate and energy risks)?
On Track: Are we on track for investment needs for paris-compliant resilient future?
Technical Efficiency: How many outputs (investment, private, public and resilient investments) do we get given the inputs? What is the unit cost and cost of finance?
Outcome: How do outcomes (greenness, resileince) compare with peers?
Policy Gap: What is the overall policy gap? (Subsidy + Public Investment Gap + Private sector policy gap)
Scenario Analysis: What is the outcome if the policy gap is overcome?

For reference here are the full climate-informed PFR questions

Macro-fiscal:
MF1: 1a What are the main climate-related (natural disaster and transition) and energy-related risks and what is their impact on the macro-economy and fiscal account? 1b What is the adequacy of fiscal buffers to protect against inevitable shocks?
MF2: What gains are there from strengthening the climate dimension of fiscal policy on the economy, fiscal-account, and climate [mitigation and adaptation]?

Climate-Fiscal Deep-Dive: (when PFR has an additional climate special chapter)
CF1: What is the fiscal cost of investment needs to transition to a Paris-compliant and resilient economy? 
CF2: How can overall long-term costs be minimized?

Expenditures: 
What is the effectiveness and efficiency of climate-related expenditures (including subsidies and public investments)?
-	E1 Climate Mitigation-Related (Green and Brown) Expenditures
-	E2 Climate Resilience and Adaptation-Related Expenditures.

Revenues:
R1: What is the impact of revenue policy on climate mitigation (and adaptation, if applicable) outcomes? 
•	Impact of taxation of fuel including carbon pricing
•	Impact of other instruments (vehicle tax/feebates, land-use fiscal instruments incl. ecological fiscal transfers, others)
R2: What is the likely impact of decarbonization and climate trends on revenue (e.g., on excises, extractives incl. minerals)?
