## Changes and why

| # | Change | Why (review point) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scope narrowed.** WP2 (project finance and real options) and WP3 (sovereign incentive and dynamic rules) are now the core. WP1 is a one-year data package. WP4 is validation only: 2 cases instead of 2-3, and about 15 interviews instead of 20-30. RQs cut from 4 to 3. | "Scope is too large for 3 years" |
| 2 | **Data named, with each quantity's status.** A table in Methods marks every quantity as estimated, calibrated, bounded or simulated. Sources: IEA Cost of Capital Observatory, IRENA (2023), Steffen (2020) methods, World Bank PPI database (multilateral-support flag), GEMs default and recovery statistics, public MDB guarantee documents, the G20 CAF review and CPAT. WP1 says plainly that H1 is an association, not a causal estimate. | "Identification and data are vague" |
| 3 | **Falsifiable hypotheses.** H1 (complementarity, estimated): the cost-of-capital reduction associated with MDB support is larger where auctions, PPAs or CfDs provide contracted revenue. H2 (threshold): a WACC reduction changes adoption only if it exceeds Δr*. Each hypothesis states what result would reject it. | "The central hypothesis is hard to falsify" |
| 4 | **Political-economy module formalised.** Adoption occurs when S + F(Δr) − λL ≥ O, a weighted-welfare model in the spirit of Grossman and Helpman (1994). S and L come from CPAT. λ is bounded by revealed preference from case histories, and results are reported across the bounded range. | "The political-economy module is underspecified" |
| 5 | **One sentence on what the project adds beyond DREI.** It makes the sovereign's policy choice endogenous and structures support over time to reward credible policy. | "Novelty against UNDP DREI" |
| 6 | **New paragraph on spatial and institutional variation and fit with the Smith School.** It ties the project to OSFG's energy-transition risk and cost-of-capital work and its India Transition Finance Programme. | "Fit with the department" |
| 7 | **Literature extended with verified sources only** (see below). The gap paragraph now cites the G20 CAF review. Recovery data (GEMs) back the point that a guarantee's cost is not its face value. Bhattacharya et al. (2016) was dropped because the text no longer cites it. | "The literature base is thin" |
| 8 | **Ethics, feasibility, supervision and timeline paragraphs** each get a bold run-in label. Feasibility now notes that the core runs on public data. The timeline and Appendix A match the narrowed work packages. | Consistency with points 1-2 |
| 9 | **Applicant paragraph** shortened and made specific: World Bank, CPAT, since 2005, and the current working papers. | Length; ai-tells (no self-praise) |
| 10 | **Full-time vs part-time** left as a marked placeholder, as asked. The supervisor sentence is kept but marked for confirmation. | "Full-time is assumed but not explained"; supervisor claim |
