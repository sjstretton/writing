# NUMBERING.md

The paper numbers follow the section folders in `C Policy and FInance` (sections 1–4) and `D Modelling` (5–7) (Stephen's restructures of 31 Oct 2026). A paper's number is its section number plus its place in the section. Folder names, file names, titles, brief series lines, citation keys, decks and the website all use these numbers.

New papers awaiting Stephen's review sit directly in `B Theory and Research` (the website's New Research tab), with no section folder: section folders exist only in the final folders, C and D. A new paper takes the next free number in the section it will join and keeps it when it moves into that section folder after review: `B Theory and Research/5.5.CarbonPriceNaturalExperiments` moves to `D Modelling/5.Modelling/` then.
