# Annotated bibliography: financing, sovereign incentives and net-zero infrastructure

Companion to `Addendum-Finance-Contracts-and-Sovereign-Balance-Sheet.md`. Compiled 1 November 2026 from web searches and pages Stephen supplied. Each entry says what the source is and what it was used for. **Read** means the page loaded in full or in substantial part; **Excerpt** means only a search-result summary was seen, so check the source before citing; **Blocked** means the page could not be opened. Quality tags: **P** peer-reviewed or official institutional; **G** grey literature or think tank; **T** trade press, advocacy or commercial (use with care). Every `[VERIFY]` in the addendum applies to the figures drawn from these sources.

## A. Supplied by Stephen

Standfirst: Norway's energy-crisis windfall should underwrite investments in developing countries. **Excerpt only** (headline, standfirst and date); the body was not seen. - Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action (2023). [Strengthening the Role of Ministries of Finance in Driving Climate Action](https://www.financeministersforclimate.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/Full%20Report%20-%20Conference%20Draft%20-%20Strengthening%20the%20Role%20of%20MoF%2020230413.pdf). ## B. Sovereign incentives and carbon markets

- World Bank (2023). [Corresponding Adjustment and Pricing of Mitigation Outcomes](https://ppp.worldbank.org/public-private-partnership/sites/default/files/2023-10/Corresponding%20Adjustment%20and%20Pricing%20of%20Mitigation%20Outcomes.pdf).
